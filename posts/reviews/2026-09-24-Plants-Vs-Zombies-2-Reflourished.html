Plants Vs Zombies 2 Reflourished (v1.6.1, 2026)

Posted on: 2026-09-24

I have always wanted to properly play through Plants Vs. Zombies 2, but any time I started a playthrough I would quickly drop off after a few worlds. This was due to a variety of factors (with one being that I don't play games on my phone often) but the biggest one really was how with every update (and so every time I'd start a new attempt) the game just kept getting... worse. I'm very thankful this mod exists as a result. And now I can give the verdict that this game is... fine?

I've always loved this games art style and premise. Unfortunately the music is not anywhere near as iconic as the original but it still sounds nice in-game. But really the main issue I have with this game is its gameplay and how the PvZ formula deteriorates slowly throughout the entire experience.

The first few worlds of the game are definitely the best ones. Everything is at its most polished and this was before PopCap had decided the specific direction they wanted to take PvZ2 levels, so they play closer to the first game (still generally shorter but a fine length) while adding all the new PvZ2 gimmicks like level challenges on top. It's all enjoyable.

At a certain point however you start seeing a very specific structure to all the worlds and the levels. The amount of levels that are solely trying to be interesting levels dramatically falls off, and instead a bunch of levels try to introduce to you the upcoming plant you'll get later in the world, or show off a premium plant to entice you to buy it (not that you can in Reflourished anyhow). The original PvZ never showed you a plant in advance for a reason. They would simply give you a plant and then give you a zombie that you could counter with it in the new few levels (It was not forced so you could experiment!).

The level length also just gets shorter the further you play. By Modern Day it doesn't even matter if I play well because even if it's a 3 flag+ level, I just need to hold on for basically a minute or 2 so that my lawn mowers can handle the rest. This very much feels like a response to the pick up and play nature of mobile devices, but it really hinders what they can do for interesting gameplay. A lot of levels just blend in together and feel like a bunch of zombies were spammed on my screen and then I killed them. There are definitely stand out levels throughout the experience, but the amount of essentially dead space due to having to make 30+ level worlds is honestly ridiculous. This ended up being the main cause of me playing this game very on and off over the past couple months.

By the way the final boss (if you can even call it that) is so disappointing. Like wow.

Not to continue to be negative, but I also have a bone to pick with the plants. Specifically the sheer quantity of them. There's too many plants and you can tell the longer you go into the game there doesn't need to really be this many more plants because the use cases become nicher and nicher, or they just do the same thing as an older plant in a slightly different way. There are definitely plants you get throughout the experience that do become an actual asset and choice to choose when figuring out how to tackle a level, but for each one of those there are 3 other plants that are basically just polluting the seed selection screen.

Now to be positive, the added content I decided to play from Reflourished was generally very good. A lot of it was better than the main game to be honest. Fun and interesting new level gimmicks, some interesting new plants and zombies (and the return of some old ones!), level lengths that can actually support these gimmicks in their entirety, and less dead levels that just feel like advertisements. I especially liked how they decided to make their own world expansions, continuing from where PopCap gave up. They feel extremely true to the original game (to the point where I didn't even realise that PopCap hadn't made most of these until I looked it up) while also being more interesting overall.

So overall, while I find PvZ2 to be a fine sequel, I also find there was a lot of room for improvement here, even if I overall did have some fun. And seriously, I'm so glad this mod is here to preserve, fix and expand what was the peak of the game. Thank you to the people who made this!

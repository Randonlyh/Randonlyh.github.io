Mina the Hollower

Posted on: 2026-11-08

If you ever wanted to experience a game with tight controls and even tighter level design, I think I found the game for you! Mina the Hollower is a blast to play through from start to finish.

The start of the game is admittedly very rough if you're not very good at souls-like combat and have not played many games with a top down view (like me), but once you start to get the hang of the controls and the main movement gimmick of this game, even though I died quite a bit (mostly on the bosses) I was still enjoying almost every minute of it. Wrap that all up with some graphics and soundtrack that are top notch for the style they were going for and it's really just a great time whether you play in short bursts or long sessions.

Really the only things holding this game down from being perfect are:

  • Some things being a bit obtuse to figure out here and there (as the game generally does just let you explore wherever which is pretty cool)
  • There is some backtracking required if you don't figure out a certain something early on which was a bit annoying (though even then this game managed to make it less annoying than I thought it would be).

But above all else though looking at everything I needed to do for 100% seemed really long and not that interesting unfortunately. I guess I can't complain too much seeing how solid the core game is though...

Play it. It's fun.

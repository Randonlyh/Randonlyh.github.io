# GMTK Theme

Countdown

## Titles

* Slithing Timing

## Ideas

Platformer where time is counting down and when it stops an event happens before counting down again

### Mechanics (on Timer end)

* Super high jump
* A jolt of speed
* Character freezes and new one spawns
* Mix them all?

#### Other Gizmos

* Clock - increases time
* Spikes
* Semi-solids
* Lava (that can move)
* Coins
* Score

# Fire Emblem: Three Houses - Cindered Shadows

It's neat overall.

Map design kinda sucked in the first half so much so that I dropped from Hard to Normal because 1 million reinforcements isn't exactly fun to react to on your first attempt... but it did get better and the general FIre Emblem gameplay is here in its usual super addicting and fun form. The new classes were cool and it was neat having way less resources at your disposal than the main game.
Story was fine. It was a neat bit of extra lore and the new characters are cool (Hapi's English VA does not seem to care at all though lol), but obviously it's not really groundbreaking storytelling because this is a DLC side mode.
Music was awesome as usual for Three Houses. No notes.

It's neat overall.
